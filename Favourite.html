Tech
Tech is making use of multiple tools such as a dash and jump, which allows you to send V1 flying through the air, or making use of a slide to get an initial boost of speed and then jumping to maintain it. Or making use of a weapon which has an alternate mode that shoots oil which takes away all friction allowing you to maintain your speed. There is also a mix of weapons such as ejecting a core from one weapon and then shooting it, causing it to explode and deal even more damage, the combination of weapons and movement is unlimited within Ultrakill.
Movement
Movement is a key part of this game, and one of the main reasons I wanted to play this game as there is no dull moment, as it can modify style (talked about later) and how you deal with enemies. The base movement you have is the ability to jump, slide, ground slam and grapple, but you can also make use of your weapons to move you around, such as the rocket launcher which shoots a rocket which you can ride around on
The weapons
The weaponry this game gives the player to use is not powerful by itself, but when used together, can be very strong and fun. All the weapons have a base and alternate modes, for example the first weapon you get is a revolver which has the 3 versions of a piercer, ricochet, and marksman. The marksman is a version of the pistol which allows you to throw coins in the air which when shot will direct the bullet to the nearest enemy's critical point, when shot when while shimmers or while falling down it will double the projectile, this projectile could be the same revolver that allowed you to throw the coin or a railcannon
Parrying
You can parry almost anything, which either returns the damage to the attacker or sends the projectile in the direction which you parried it. A parry occurs when you punch the attack or projectile at the right distance from the player. The parry mechanic doesn't stop at just enemy attacks but also your own projectiles, if you fire your shotgun and parry at the right time you increase the velocity of your shotgun bullets which then explode on whatever they hit. You can also parry the cannon ball that is shot out of the alternate version of the rocket launcher allowing you to instantly kill one of the tankier enemies in the game
Community
Even though the game is a gruesome run and gun, the community is a rather goofy bunch and so are the developers, compared to the diehard heavy-metal Doom community, I'll talk more about the developers as they include a large number of funny easter eggs in the game such as florp, florp is a joke about hampter. Another easter egg is KITR, which is a cat found in the levels which can instantly kill any enemy when it comes in contact with it. Not only are the easter eggs the only goofy things in the game but also some challenges the game provides such as 'Beat the level without touching water' in a water based level or the secret levels which are unlocked by finding secret orbs which can be an entirely different game. For example one of the secret levels is a fishing level and another is a Crash Bandicoot level which is a very big change while playing through the levels. There is a credit level which replaces the normal slow scroll credits and is instead a museum of all the developers, who are shown as little plushies, and some history of the first versions of the weapons. If you take these plushies and throw them into the trash can around the museum it will play a low res explosion sound effect.
