The idea
The world in which Ultrakill takes place is the earth, our world, however, in this world people were at war for so many years where they continue to create machines and innovate new ways of fighting
The machines
This explains where all of machine-based enemies come from in the game, such as the Gutterman. This was the first machine made, but the war continued and so did the innovations, the order in which the machines were made is unknown but V1 is also one of these machines, all of these machines are powered by blood
When it went wrong
During all these innovations of war and machines people discovered Hell, where the rest of the enemies come from. This is where we find ourselves going through the different layers of hell not for a purpose not to destroy the great evil, the machine we play does not devolop, it only kills and gains fuel as hordes of demons and outcasts from heaven, Heaven also exists in this; however, it has fallen apart.
Gabriel
One of the many bosses we fight in this game, is Gabriel, an angel that came to stop us from the heavens with the title of God's will, the first time we fight him he has no second thought of loss, he knows he will win. However the machine made to kill, V1 wins, but the angel doesn't die but rather retreats, defeated in a rage, back to the council, unwelcomed and shamed for being defeated by a machine. The council gives him a day to unmake his mistake, and if not, he will be banished. We learn Gabriel has devoted himself entirely to God, however after fighting him for a second time he returns to the council but not in sorrow but rage as he begins to kill them, we see a shift in his character, character devolopment you could say, this has brought the idea that he is the main character in the story

Terminals
The terminals are what allow us to travel between the different levels in the game, by making use of an elevator shaft, which moves us between the levels. We know that the terminals are sentient and are bored thus the reason they take us to the different levels, as they find this entertaining, they even invented a nonstop kill box that summons enemies as the stages are cleared. We learn in the new layer why they play this anonymous song every time you interact with a terminal, as it was the only media used to test the teleportation technology of the terminals to find out whether it works, and is now lost and plays endlessly.
Recent
In the most recent update we learn about the operating system that was used amongst the people and how it was upgraded as time went on, these operating system also run on blood, The main name of this operating system were smileOS, which the design of the website is based on. Which went through two iterations before the Nocturnal systems were made, another operating system which is made of a cluster of various wartime machine operating systems. This Nocturnal system has an unknown amount of resource requirements and was never meant to be released.
We also find a statue of God in this layer holding a small spinning earth on the tip of his finger in a dark room, this shows us that there is a physical being that has all this power, which is limited as he can't do everything he pleases.
